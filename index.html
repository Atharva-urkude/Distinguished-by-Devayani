Our story · A kitchen in Pune

Some traditions are best shared by the spoonful.

Délisse began with a love for the gentle contrast at the heart of tiramisu: coffee-soaked biscuits, soft mascarpone cream, and a final veil of cocoa. We bring that Italian favourite to Pune, making each order with patience and a little everyday joy.

From a small tasting cup to a box for the whole table, every format is made to make sharing feel special. Choose your size, gather your people, and leave room for one more spoonful.
